Products

At Iron Tech, we specialize exclusively in the manufacturing of Rotogravure Printing Cylinders — delivering precision, durability, and world-class performance for the printing industry. With years of expertise and advanced technology, we ensure every cylinder meets international standards of quality and accuracy. Our mission is to support our clients with reliable, high-performance cylinders that enhance print quality and production efficiency. At Iron Tech, excellence is engraved in every detail.

Rotogravure Cylinders

Our rotogravure cylinders are engineered with precision and durability to deliver consistent, high-quality printing results. Manufactured using advanced CNC technologies — including laser engraving, plasma cutting, and CNC lathes — each cylinder meets international standards for accuracy, balance, and surface quality. At Iron Tech, we ensure every cylinder contributes to sharper prints, longer lifespan, and superior performance for the packaging and printing industries.
