Our Business Units

At Iron Tech, we specialize exclusively in the manufacturing of Rotogravure Printing Cylinders — delivering precision, durability, and world-class performance for the printing industry. With years of expertise and advanced technology, we ensure every cylinder meets international standards of quality and accuracy. Our mission is to support our clients with reliable, high-performance cylinders that enhance print quality and production efficiency. At Iron Tech, excellence is engraved in every detail.

Printing Cylinder Manufacturing

Manufacturing high-precision Rotogravure and Flexographic Printing Cylinders according to customer specifications.

Metal Machining & Raw Material Supply

Conventional and CNC machining for various industrial applications, along with the supply of machining raw materials, including steel pipes, billets, and bars.

Steel Structures & Steel Sections

Manufacturing and fabrication of steel structures and metal constructions, with the supply of various types and sizes of steel sections.

CNC Cutting & Steel Plate Supply

High-precision CNC Laser and Plasma Cutting services, with the supply of steel sheets and plates in various grades and thicknesses.
